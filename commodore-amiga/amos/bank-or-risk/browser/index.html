Keyboard: R to roll, B to bank, Space to start or continue, A to play again, Q to end.

Your rounds

Your secured points will appear here.

    Keep it, or risk it?

    Roll to add 1–6 to your current total. Bank to secure those points and finish the round. Go over 10 and that round earns zero. Exactly 10 banks automatically.

    Reach 30 secured points within five rounds. A bust keeps your earlier points. If the rounds left cannot get you to 30, the challenge ends early. Take your time: there’s no timer.
