What changed?

The marker spans positions 520–600. At a wall, position is clamped and velocity becomes zero; the ships do not bounce. Positive velocity means right, negative means left.

Inspect one update

For each 1/60-second step, input selects a target velocity: left = −top speed, released = 0, right = +top speed. Velocity approaches that target by acceleration × time while a direction is held, or braking × time after release. Position then increases by velocity × time.

Acceleration and braking are measured in units/s². This is a chosen control model, not a complete physics simulation. The three presets change several settings to make the contrast clear; compare the numerical rules before attributing a difference to just one.
